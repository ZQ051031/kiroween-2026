# Haunted Hollow build sequence

- Define room data, evidence and item IDs, seal requirements, and scoring (requirements 1–3).
- Draw the three room illustrations and implement the room navigation, accessible landmarks, Journal, and narrative ending (1–3, 6).
- Wire the typed story choices and party checklist (4).
- Add versioned local progress, confirmation-based reset, and optional synthesized ambience (5–6).
- Exercise the wrong and right answer paths in every room, repeat discoveries, reload progress, reset, generate a story, toggle sound and tasks, and check layout with keyboard, mobile, and reduced motion (1–6).
